About the club

The club gives children and young people a place for dance, physical development, creativity, and learning about the cultures of many peoples.

Our goals

  • Regular dance classes and physical training.
  • Learning about cultural traditions from around the world.
  • Building confidence, discipline, and teamwork.
  • Taking part in community and volunteer projects.
