# Programs and Community Work

## Class packages

### Full-Performance Package

Boys pay $250 per month. Girls pay $350 per month. The difference in price is due to the difference in cost for costume cleaning after performances.

This package includes participation in performances, costume rental, and access to all classes from the schedule. Students who register before the end of September get June free.

### Single Class

The cost is $40 per class. This package does not include performances or costume rental. Students can discuss a transition to the full-performance package or the twice-weekly package with the director. In that case, the $40 for the class will be returned.

### Twice-Weekly Package

The cost is $250 per month. This package includes two classes per week for one month, but does not include performances or costume rental. The same price applies to boys and girls.

### Parents Group

The cost is $30 per class on Fridays from 6:30 to 8:00 pm. This package is for the moms and dads of children and youth who are already enrolled in the club on a full-performance or twice-weekly package.

## Class schedule

- **Monday to Thursday, 6:00 to 8:00 pm:** Open class, available to all students
- **Friday, 5:00 to 6:30 pm:** Group 1, available to all students
- **Friday, 6:30 to 8:00 pm:** Group 2, available only to students approved by the director

Most classes take place at the studio at 217 Brighton Beach Ave.

## Projects

### Dance Under The Sky

This annual street festival brings together over 20 dance groups to celebrate folk traditions and diversity in South Brooklyn. Families, schools, and communities gather for performances and cultural exchange in an open atmosphere.

The event features folk dances and live music from around the world, highlighting different cultures coming together on one stage.

### Summer Camp

The summer camp takes children outside of New York City to learn folk dances, explore new skills, and enjoy recreational activities in nature. The camp encourages children to try new experiences, build independence, and discover their strengths.

Camp combines structured learning with play and exploration. Children grow through teamwork, cultural appreciation, and shared activities.

### Afterschool Program

Folk Dance Club Kaleidoscope is a DOE vendor providing folk dance and cultural programs in New York City public schools. Students learn traditional dances from around the world while gaining confidence, teamwork skills, and cultural appreciation.

Each program ends with public showcases at school events and student participation in the NYC Dance Parade.

## Other community work

The club's projects include the Kaleidoscope of History project, in which students learn about World War II and perform war dances in honor of Victory Day. The dancers also perform for elderly people in nursing homes and offer free New Year's themed performances across New York City each year.

The club does little traditional advertising. Many new participants hear about it through dancers, parents, and public performances.

## Registration

Families can register a child for classes, performances, or community programs. The registration process asks for dancer information, an emergency contact, medical information, a payment arrangement, and additional information. Families also review the club's rules and permissions before submitting an electronic signature.

Children and youth of different backgrounds and experience levels are welcome. No prior dance experience is required. Families can contact the club to discuss class placement, schedule, and next steps.
